# Downloaded Papers

## Core Theory & Methodology

1. **The Linear Representation Hypothesis and the Geometry of Large Language Models**
   - File: `2311.03658_linear_representation_hypothesis.pdf`
   - Authors: Park, Choe, Veitch
   - Year: 2024 (ICML)
   - arXiv: 2311.03658
   - Why relevant: Core theoretical framework for concept directions in LLMs. Defines causal inner product, proves connection between probing and steering.

2. **The Geometry of Truth: Emergent Linear Structure in LLM Representations**
   - File: `2310.06824_representation_engineering.pdf` (note: filename is misleading, this is Marks & Tegmark)
   - Authors: Marks, Tegmark
   - Year: 2024 (COLM)
   - arXiv: 2310.06824
   - Why relevant: Mass-mean probing methodology, activation patching for localization, causal validation via intervention. Most directly applicable methodology.

3. **Representation Engineering: A Top-Down Approach to AI Transparency**
   - File: `2310.01405_representation_engineering_zou.pdf`
   - Authors: Zou et al.
   - Year: 2023
   - arXiv: 2310.01405
   - Why relevant: Contrastive stimuli approach for finding concept directions. Reading vectors for concept detection.

4. **Locating and Editing Factual Associations in GPT (ROME)**
   - File: `2202.05262_locating_editing_factual_associations.pdf`
   - Authors: Meng, Bau, Andonian, Belinkov
   - Year: 2022 (NeurIPS)
   - arXiv: 2202.05262
   - Why relevant: Shows factual knowledge is in middle-layer MLPs at last subject token. Causal tracing methodology.
